## If the protocol is explicitly made feasible

First identify the assigned method lane and agree on the registry definition; do not silently omit the dense witness. Restore and hash-check the shared template, stack, actual footprint, segment geometry, catalogue and registry. Verify official source metadata and permitted use. Freeze a comparable incumbent and folds. Within at most three experiments or two hours: run every feature's single-feature holdout AUC; stop at AUC > 0.90 unless leakage is disproven; build every catalogue feature from visible faults only; hide complete segments with buffers; mask visible faults exactly; calculate pooled HOLDOUT-DTI and paired spatial-block 95% CI with the shared evaluator. Measure surface and final-dot overlap/correlation against the agreed full registry. Reject invalid outputs before using the shared writer, reopen saved bytes, and check geometry and values. Only then may a separate selector decide whether to use a real slot. Holdout is not a leaderboard forecast.
