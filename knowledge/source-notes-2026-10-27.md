## Official sources and implications

- [DrivenData problem and metric](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/): the target is geological fault probability, not geothermal vent prediction. One float32 band, EPSG:32611, 100 m resolution, same training bounds, values [0,1]; the wording says data outside the bounds is null/NaN. Distance-weighted Tversky uses α=0.2, β=0.8 and a triangular 300 m kernel.
- [DrivenData staff on known-fault masking](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516): pixels corresponding to known USGS/INGENIOUS faults are excluded in both evaluations. This does **not** establish that all neighboring pixels are excluded.
- [DrivenData staff on pooling](https://community.drivendata.org/t/leaderboard-aggregation-pooled-over-public-test-pixels-or-mean-of-per-chunk-scores/11550): public and private scores pool their respective pixels into one index; the final re-evaluation uses the entire GeoDAWN area.
- [Competition homepage](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/) displayed a December 3, 2026, 23:59 UTC end. The [official rules PDF](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf) directs readers to the competition site for the current timeline but its general Appendix A mentions 5 p.m. ET on the deadline date. These may apply to different aspects; **confirm with organizers and do not assume either resolves the discrepancy**. Rules state three submissions/week, one final selection for both rounds, reproducible solution assets, license rights, eligibility conditions, and disclosure of the extent/how generative AI was used in the narrative.
- [Public leaderboard](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/leaderboard/) displayed `xiaofanhu` at 0.4544 when read around 17:26 UTC. This is another participant's public score, not this project's estimate or a receipt for any file. The scheduled feed preserves the last observation and marks refresh failure or age as stale.
- [DrivenData data tab](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/data/) redirected to login. The restored sample, fault raster and feature stack are public owner mirrors, hash-verified to pins but not authenticated as organizer downloads. The mirror's sample contains positive values on known faults although the public description calls the sample total fault absence; it is used here for grid/footprint checks, not as geological truth.
- [USGS GeoDAWN data release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) describes magnetic/radiometric flight data and GeoTIFF grids under CC0; variable terrain clearance and differing flight specifications are important acquisition confounders. The original raw AOI files have not been downloaded in this session.
- [USGS 3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program) states its products are free and without use restrictions. Exact 1 m AOI tiles have not been verified or obtained here.
- [DOE GDR INGENIOUS 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) publicly lists 2 m probe surveys and well/spring temperatures under CC BY 4.0 with attribution. The relevant raw files and coverage have not been independently downloaded here.
