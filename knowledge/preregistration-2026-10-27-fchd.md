## 1. PhD Synthesis of Prior `WBGGEMSDOE3` Lanes & Why `GEMSDOE32` (`0.2778`) Led Focus-9

### 1.1 Exact Algebraic Reason `GEMSDOE32` (`0.2778` owner-reported) Topped Focus-9
Byte-level inspection of the Focus-9 submission rasters establishes an exact subset chain:
- `19GEMSDOE` `h19-5` (`121,131` px, `0.1922`) $\supset$ `GEMSDOE25`/`GEMSDOE30` (`44,090` px, `0.2600`) $\supset$ `GEMSDOE31` `d2.8` (`40,199` px, `0.2708`) $\supset$ **`GEMSDOE32` `B2` (`37,654` px, `0.2778`)**.
- Under the GEMS Distance-Weighted Tversky Index ($\alpha = 0.2, \beta = 0.8$), known catalogue faults $F_{\text{cat}}$ (`100,192` px) are masked from both prediction and hidden test labels $G_{\text{hid}}$, and each predicted pixel $x \notin F_{\text{cat}}$ changes DTI positively iff its expected 300 m kernel credit satisfies:
  $$\Delta \mathrm{TP}_w(x) > \frac{\alpha\,\mathrm{DTI}}{1 - \alpha\,\mathrm{DTI}} \approx 0.059\text{ at }\mathrm{DTI}=0.2778.$$
- `GEMSDOE32` `B2` stripped exactly `2,545` near-catalogue collar pixels ($\le 2\text{ px}$ from $F_{\text{cat}}$) from `GEMSDOE31` `d2.8`. Because mapped faults are masked at evaluation and adjacent sub-200 m pixels lie inside mapped scarp zones rather than unmapped fault strands, removing those `2,545` zero-return false positives shrank the $0.2\,\mathrm{FP}_w$ denominator penalty by ~`509` weighted units while losing negligible $\mathrm{TP}_w$, lifting DTI from `0.2708` to `0.2778` (`+0.0070`).
- Conversely, Focus-9 sites share `86.7%–100%` 3-px dot overlap with each other (`GEMSDOE25` and `GEMSDOE30` are byte-identical `5b9ba892...`), so perturbing `d2.8`/`B2` violates the Parallel-Run uniqueness gate (`<= 0.70` 3-px overlap).

## 3. Single-Lane Execution Plan (`FCHD-NCM`) — Strict 3-Experiment Budget

All 3 experiments execute inside the single `FCHD-NCM` lane on `GEMSDOE32-HIDE-RECOVER-v3.0` (`22,232` withheld fault pixels across 8 quadrant-draw folds, `15 px` spatial training collar, `3 px` visible-fault evaluation exclusion):

1. **`E1` (`FCHD-ZeroCat-OOF`)**:
   - **Inputs**: Strictly zero-catalogue 62-feature physical stack (`dem10_chi`, `topo_10m`, Lahontan `zmean_m` shoreline deconfounder, BLM claim & TIGER road deconfounders, 19-band `training_features` with `IR-20` decoded float32 `tc`, `geodawn_rad_u8`, `geodawn_extensions_u8` 0→150 m worm persistence, `lidar_scarp_features_u8`, and GDR #1391 2 m probe / sinter / geothermometry).
   - **Training**: Spatially blocked 4-quadrant OOF `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (`train_zone = foot & ~collar`) using **Relief-Matched Hard-Negative Contrastive Sampling** (`50%` relief/slope-matched negatives + `35%` Lahontan/BLM/road mimic hard negatives + `15%` uniform basin/range negatives) so the model learns *tectonic vs non-tectonic* scarps rather than mountain elevation/slope.
   - **Allocation**: Dinkelbach-calibrated Contrarian Submodular CELF (`crowd_cover` exclusion against dense registry masks) enforcing pre-placement `|Spearman| <= 0.90` and post-placement non-lattice 3-px overlap `<= 0.70`.

2. **`E2` (`FCHD-NCM-Struct-OOF`)**:
   - **Inputs**: `E1`'s 62 zero-catalogue physical features + **10 strictly fold-local Nested Component-Masked (`NCM`) structural-mechanics features** (fault-tip PCA propagation lobes, dilatational en-échelon relay corridors, antithetic/piedmont step-out band `6–38 px`, and regional strain-aligned strike continuation).
   - **Strict Zero-Leakage Guarantee**: During fold training on `train_zone = foot & ~collar`, connected components of `cell.visible_full & train_zone` are partitioned into 4 nested component folds; structural features for positives on component fold $m$ (and matched negatives) are computed **only from the remaining 3/4 of visible training components**! At fold test inference and full export, structural features are computed strictly from `cell.visible_full` (`existing_faults.tif` at export) with zero access to `ctx.labels`. This eliminates both `IR-25` spatial collar leakage and `IR-11` self-distance collapse (`-d_vis` raw proximity is NEVER included as a raw monotone ring; every structural feature is out-of-component masked!).

3. **`E3` (`FCHD-DualRegime-Balanced-OOF`)**:
   - **Mechanism**: Out-of-fold calibrated log-odds synthesis of **Regime A (`E1` pure zero-catalogue off-trace discovery)** and **Regime B (`E2` nested component-masked structural-physical continuation)**, with blend weight $\lambda \in \{0.25, 0.35, 0.45, 0.55\}$ selected on Draw-20 folds and verified out-of-sample on Draw-21 folds, followed by **Dual-Quota Contrarian DTI-Optimal Submodular Allocation** (guaranteeing dedicated off-catalogue basin/piedmont discovery coverage $> 6\text{ px}$ from mapped faults alongside high-precision tip/relay continuations, with optimal dot budget $K^* \in [28\,000, 46\,000]$ determined by Dinkelbach marginal DTI gain $> 0$).
