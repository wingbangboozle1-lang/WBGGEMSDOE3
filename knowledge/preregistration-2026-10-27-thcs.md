## 1. Verified Data & Newly Uncovered Irregularities (Pre-Implementation Audit)

All 26 competition and public external datasets in `data/raw/` and `data/raw/external/`, 118 ledger GeoTIFFs in `data/ledger/`, and 12 peer GeoTIFFs in `data/peer_extra/` were SHA-256 verified on disk prior to designing hypotheses:

- **`IR-20` (Organizer GeoTIFF Tag vs. Physical Pixel Content on `training_features.tif` Band 6 `tc`):**  
  Cell 5 of the official reference notebook (`official-unet-mc-cv-reference-solution.ipynb`) prints the embedded GeoTIFF description for Band 6 (`tc`): *"Tilt angle or total curvature - magnetic field derivative for edge detection (Category: magnetic_data)"*. By contrast, the official DrivenData Problem Description (`https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/`, figure `gems_tc_tmi.png`) captions `tc` as *"total radiometric counts per second"*. Direct pixel-by-pixel verification over all `5,164,300` valid overlap cells between `training_features.tif` Band 6 (`tc`, range `2.95–88.57`) and USGS GeoDAWN radiometric Total Count (`geodawn_rad_u8.tif` Band 4 `TC`, `DOI: 10.5066/P93LGLVQ`) yields **`Spearman ρ = 0.999953`** (`ρ = +0.8858` with Potassium `K`; `ρ = -0.0290` with `TMI`). Prior lanes in this repository misidentified Band 6 as either magnetic tilt curvature (`CSPES`) or borehole thermal conductivity (`deep-research-2026-10-27.md`), leaving **both** full-precision float32 gamma-ray radiometrics and **true** magnetic tilt angle $\theta_{\text{mag}} = \arctan(\text{tmi\_vg} / (\text{tmi\_hg} + \epsilon))$ unexploited.
- **`IR-21` (Dimensional Violation in `src/gemsdoe/validation.py`):**  
  `score_from_profile(h, mass_, n_truth)` multiplied unscaled `62,122`-px SGMC hit rate `h = TP_sgmc / mass` by `mass_` against `n_truth = 15,672.5`, violating the DTI upper bound `0 <= TP_w <= |G|`, predicting `0.3992` for the `0.0904` uniform lattice (silently trimmed out as an "outlier" by `robust=True`), and inflating `skarpline-union` (`97,608` dots) to `0.4284`. Fixed upstream so `TP` scales by prevalence (`capture * n_truth <= n_truth`).
- **`IR-22` (SGMC Bedrock Contact Trap & Catalogue-Continuation Overfitting Trap):**  
  Across `122` unique SHA-256 live-scored GeoTIFFs verified on disk, direct submissions of off-catalogue SGMC faults (`GEMSDOE29` `sgmc-off-catalogue-44k`: `0.0512`; `GEMSDOE72` `h72-1-sgmc-offcatalogue`: `0.0492`) failed because `SGMC \ cat` (`62,122` px) is dominated by inactive Paleozoic/Mesozoic bedrock contacts. Conversely, feeding `existing_faults` proximity/tip-continuation as model features (`GEMSDOE76` `H76D`: `0.1580` holdout -> `0.1555` live vs zero-catalogue `G76C`: `0.2343` live; `HCE` `E2/E3`: `0.0236`) overfits synthetic cut-tips in hide-and-recover. Consequently, **all features in `THCS-OOF` are strictly zero-catalogue** (`existing_faults` is never read by feature extraction).
- **`IR-23` (GitHub Pages Dual-Banner & Redirect Defect):**  
  `docs/index.html` and `docs/executive-summary.html` simultaneously advertised two conflicting green download CTAs (`wbggems3-hce1-latest.tif` and `wbggemsdoe3-skarpline-union-20261027-610acf58-zeros.tif`), while `docs/submission.html` was a redirect stub. Resolved in our unified Pages overhaul.

   Every individual input feature in `THCS-OOF` is evaluated standalone by ROC-AUC against withheld fault positives across the 8 spatial holdout folds before any model training. **Hard Gate:** `max_j max(AUC_j, 1 - AUC_j) <= 0.90`. Any feature exceeding `0.90` halts execution as suspected catalogue leakage.
2. **Experiment `E1` — Zero-Catalogue Cover-Stratified Geophysical + True Tilt + Float32 Radiometric + Road-Damped LiDAR OOF HGB + 300 m CELF (`N = 38,000`, `b_visible = 2.0 px`):**

   Identical OOF split, hyperparameters, and CELF placement (`N = 38,000`, `b_visible = 2.0 px`) to isolate the exact `HOLDOUT-DTI` ablation delta of GDR #1391 hydrothermal data.
4. **Experiment `E3` — Variance-Reduced Multi-Capacity OOF Ensemble + Optimal DTI Marginal-Gain Stopping Budget (`N*` via Inner-Fold CV):**  
   Combines the stronger feature set (`max(E1, E2)`) across a variance-reduced 2-member OOF ensemble (regularized HGB depth-6 + interaction-focused HGB depth-4 with extra-trees / L2 smoothing + strike-anisotropic ridge sharpening along the predicted probability crest) and selects the optimal CELF dot budget `N* in {34000, 38000, 42000, 46000}` strictly by inner out-of-fold marginal-gain optimality (Theorem: bang-bang binary `{0.0, 1.0}` amplitudes maximize linear-fractional DTI over `[0, 1]^N` for 300 m kernel-separated centers).
