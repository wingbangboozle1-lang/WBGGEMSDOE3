## 5. The "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]" error

- The eight surveyed TIFFs have zero finite values outside [0,1] (R12); this does not reproduce portal handling of NaNs or other encodings.
- Two archived stand-ins have the wrong grid: 1200 × 1600 and 1024 × 1024 instead of the mirrored 3730 × 3292 grid. The portal error mapping and identity of the rejected bytes are not known, so their contribution to the reported error cannot be established.
- Untested hypotheses: NaN outside the footprint without a nodata tag; a sentinel value; float rounding
  above 1.0. Only a portal test with an authenticated account can distinguish them. Flagged as IR-14.
