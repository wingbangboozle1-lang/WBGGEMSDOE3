## 1. Why a new signal family, not another prune of the dotted-ridge family

Every one of the nine requested focus sites' top owner-reported scores (GEMSDOE24/25/27/28/30/31/32/33/36, `0.2449`–`0.2778`) is a pruning or re-weighting variant of the same dotted-ridge/topo-gap-closure surface family — confirmed by their mutual >97% 3-pixel dot overlap in `registry/focus_sites.json` and this repo's own uniqueness checks. GEMSDOE32's `0.2778` in particular is explained by removing 2,545 near-catalogue-collar pixels from the GEMSDOE31/28 base (see `docs/executive-summary.html`, "Why GEMSDOE32?"): under the α=0.2/β=0.8 distance-weighted Tversky metric, mapped faults are masked from scoring, so false positives within a few pixels of the catalogue are almost pure penalty — pruning them is a nearly-free DTI gain, not evidence of a better underlying signal. Continuing to prune that same family offers shrinking, already-mined returns and would also fail this repo's own uniqueness gate against the many prior lanes already derived from it. The hypotheses below instead target signal planes not used by any of those files.

## 3. Frozen promotion gates (checked in `evidence/smsr_run_card.json`)

A candidate from this lane is marked `promote-to-selector` (never `auto-submit` — promotion is a separate human selector step) only if, in code, **all** of the following hold:

1. **Leakage canary**: no newly-added SMSR plane individually exceeds AUC `0.90` on the hide-and-recover holdout. (The inherited base features `d`/`d_perp`, present in every `gems57` configuration including the shipped baseline, are flagged at `0.9245`–`0.9353` in both arms — a known, previously documented property of this exact evaluator design, not introduced by SMSR, and reported rather than hidden because it could still affect the absolute DTI level even though it cancels in the SMSR-vs-shipped differential.)
2. **Holdout DTI**: detached-mode SMSR DTI exceeds the shipped-feature baseline with a reported 95% quadrant-jackknife CI (`+0.00395`, CI `[0.2789, 0.3637]`); the all-mode result is reported alongside even though it disagrees in sign (`−0.01048`), per the no-cherry-picking requirement.
3. **Uniqueness**: `max dot_overlap_3px ≤ 0.70` OR `max |Spearman ρ| ≤ 0.90` against all 9 focus sites and all prior `WBGGEMSDOE3` lanes (observed max overlap `0.516`, max |ρ| `0.0197` — both comfortably inside bounds) — **except** the dense, 100%-footprint `17GEMSDOE_E-proba-multiscale` witness, where the literal overlap test reads `1.0` (mathematically guaranteed against any dense all-positive comparator) while the rank-correlation test reads `ρ=0.094` (clear pass). Both readings are reported together; this disagreement is an explicit, unresolved irregularity flagged for the human selector, not silently decided by this project.
4. **Format/range contract**: single-band float32, EPSG:32611, shape matching `sample_submission.tif` exactly, zero NaN/Inf anywhere, all in-footprint values in `[0.0, 1.0]`, zero on the mapped-catalogue mask — verified by re-opening the saved file (not just the in-memory array) via the template's `submission_writer.write_submission()`.
5. **Budget discipline**: ≤ 3 experiments per lane; `evidence/smsr_run_card.json` records `2` experiments run, `0` submission slots spent by this session.
