# Pre-implementation hypotheses and lane declaration

Recorded 2026-10-26 UTC, before implementing or fitting a new model. This repository initially had no model or geological feature code. The priority sites contain many earlier ideas; we cannot prove an idea has never appeared anywhere in all parallel repositories. These are **proposals**, not discoveries or measured improvements. Expected benefit is a qualitative ranking, not a score. A feature-only leakage canary, the pinned shared holdout, the complete literal registry checks and a paired win against the applicable best control are prerequisites to any promotion.

## Preregistered attempt and stop

The provisional single method lane is **TAD-GEO**; no combination with the other proposals is authorized in this run. Before any placement, the independently checked registered lattice gives a deterministic impossibility bound at the historical 37,654-dot budget: at most 6,649 of the 5,167,373 footprint cells are farther than 3 pixels from that raster. Consequently any such candidate must have at least 82.34% of its dots within 3 pixels of that one registered raster, exceeding the literal 70% limit. See [`evidence/run-card.json`](../evidence/run-card.json) and [`docs/research.html`](../docs/research.html).

This is a **pre-placement duplicate STOP**, not a failed numerical experiment and not proof for every smaller dot budget. Surface correlation cannot be measured without the missing cached surfaces, and no canary, candidate fit, holdout or new TIFF was produced. Constraining points to the few lattice holes merely to game novelty would be an unvalidated change of method, not evidence of geothermal discovery. Do not silently drop the lattice or reinterpret the user's rule. A lower-budget geological method would need a separately preregistered, scientifically motivated budget, full pre-placement registry checks and the same holdout gates.
