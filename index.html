Why the historic H33-B2 result is useful—but not submit-ready

The owner-maintained record describes H33-B2 as a sparser, catalogue-buffered derivative of a shared H19-5/D2.8 lineament family. That is consistent with the DTI objective: redundant dots with little marginal hidden-fault credit can add false-positive cost, while removing them can improve the ratio. It is not evidence that the method finds new fault structures, and its reported score is not independently tied to a portal receipt in the reviewed files.

Every one of the nine historic site winners is marked non-unique or closely nested in the owner audit. The H33 TIFF is referenced for forensic learning only; it is not copied or linked as a download here.

How this workbench stays honest

Holdout metric
Only a fold-local hide-and-recover result counts as HOLDOUT-DTI; it must name the evaluator, withheld-positive count, and confidence interval.
Leaderboard score
Use ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED only from a visible submission-page receipt. Historical scores without a byte-linked receipt are marked OWNER-REPORTED (unverified).
Novelty gate
Check the continuous surface before placement and final dots against the full registry; stop on the specified correlation or three-pixel overlap thresholds.
Submission banner
Only a machine-gated `promote` result may be labeled submit-ready. Current state is negative and the download control is disabled.
