01 · Proposed lane / medium cost

Cross-scale potential-field edge stability

Prespecified gradient alignment and persistence in gravity and reduced-to-pole magnetics, conditioned on detrended elevation. Buried offsets could lack a visible scarp. Mimic: lithologic contacts or survey stripes. Uses the cached 19-band mirror.

02 · Data blocked / high cost

Consistent displaced channel corridors

Use 3DEP DEM and drainage lines to look for several coherent offsets across one lineament. Mimic: erosion, fans and artificial diversions. The free official source is known; competition-tile availability here is not verified.

03 · Alternative / medium cost

Gravity–magnetic edge disagreement

Examine scale-stable density boundaries without a matching magnetic boundary, conditioned on geodetic shear. Mimic: nonmagnetic lithologic contacts and interpolation. Related potential-field work already exists.

04 · Data blocked / high cost

Thermal contrast, normalized by sampling

Compare INGENIOUS probe and spring temperatures to local sampling effort and structure. Mimic: groundwater flow or biased sampling. The public GDR listing has CC BY 4.0 data; bytes and local coverage remain unchecked.

Faults are the target, not vents.

The competition asks for surface fault probabilities. Faults can create pathways for geothermal fluids, but neither a magnetic edge nor a mapped fault establishes a vent or commercially viable resource.

The official GeoDAWN survey explains variable aircraft terrain clearance and different acquisition spacings. Cross-scale signals may reflect acquisition and processing, not tectonics; the model must test those confounders.
