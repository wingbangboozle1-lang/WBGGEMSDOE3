INTERPRETATION

A plausible metric mechanism.

The official metric uses a triangular 300 m kernel, α = 0.2 and β = 0.8. Spacing dots can retain partial credit for nearby truth while reducing false-positive mass; pruning low-yield dots may improve the index.

With weighted true positives T, false positives F and fixed truth count N, the denominator is 0.2F + 0.8N + 0.2T. A removal helps only when the decrease in false-positive penalty outweighs its lost true-positive credit. Nearby mapped faults are excluded pixel-exactly according to DrivenData staff.

The historical 0.2778 figure is user/owner-reported, not an organizer receipt. It cannot prove which removed pixels were false positives, nor that this approach wins on the private or expanded labels.

02 / BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION

Four hypotheses, ranked.

Rank is qualitative expected value and cost, not a measured score. None was fitted in this run.

01

Thermal disequilibrium at contacts

Repeated GDR site observations: compare geothermometer estimates with observed water temperature, then locate support near independent gravity/magnetic contact intersections. Potential blind upflow signal; water mixing, lithology and sampling bias are mimics.

PROVISIONAL LANE · MEDIUM COST · CACHE BLOCKED
02

Time-varying displacement steps

NASA OPERA displacement time series plus persistent 3DEP scarps may reveal motion across covered structures. Pumping, atmosphere and coherence can imitate a tectonic step. Specific tiles and access are not yet verified.

HIGH COST · NOT YET RUNNABLE
03

Signed gravity–magnetic offsets

Compare normal-direction gradient peaks across scales rather than simply adding field amplitudes. A buried basin margin may shift density and magnetization boundaries; dikes or lithologic contacts are alternatives.

MEDIUM COST · CACHE BLOCKED
04

Coherent drainage offsets

Test whether multiple catchments show consistent signed channel offsets across a persistent slope break. Fan switching, landslides, shoreline benches and roads can look similar.

HIGH COST · TILE LIST UNVERIFIED

03 / IRREGULARITIES

What could mislead us.

Failing closed means recording both evidence and its boundaries.

01

Registered lattice makes the historical budget infeasible.

The 695-entry pinned registry includes a calibration raster whose three-pixel halo covers 99.87% of the mirrored footprint. At 37,654 dots, the minimum possible overlap is 82.34%. This does not prove that every smaller-budget method is impossible, nor does it authorize gaming the holes.

02

The mirrored sample is not the described zero sample.

Its 60,988 positive cells exactly match the mirrored known-fault raster, whereas the official page describes a total-absence sample. Provenance is not authenticated. Read its validity mask and grid only—never its values as features, truth, or predictions.

03

Range validity and outside-nodata conventions differ.

The shared pinned writer emits finite zeros outside without an internal mask. Our conservative local validator also requires the outside to be masked, so the two policies do not currently produce a passing end-to-end artifact. This is a local caution, not proof that the portal rejects the writer. No private fork or unverified “fix” was made.

04

Shared metric prose has an over-broad bound.

The pinned metric module's prose says marginal truth credit is always at most the dot's nearest-truth credit. A single dot can cover multiple truth cells, so the sum can exceed that maximum. The default-parameter arithmetic has not been shown wrong by this observation. Upstream write access is unavailable; we report the issue rather than silently maintain a private fork.

05

Scores, data and permissions have different provenance.

Leaderboard claims in the nine-site table have no organizer receipts; the current public leaderboard is a separate dated observation. The input files are hash-pinned owner mirrors, not authenticated by DrivenData. The cached band arrays, original DEM link list, hidden truth and team quota are missing. GitHub reports no identified license for the shared owner code.
