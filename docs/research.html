Rank 1 · H1-RMIX · NOT TESTED

Local radiometric mixing-manifold residual at concealed structures

Layers: GeoDAWN K, GeoDAWN Th, GeoDAWN U, GeoDAWN total count, potential-field edge context, cover/relief strata

Mechanism: Hydrothermal alteration and fluid-rock interaction may alter the local K-Th-U-total-count composition; a buried structural boundary may preserve a narrow compositional discontinuity where surface scarps are muted.

Signature: Robust local compositional log-ratio closure/manifold residual, evaluated for multiscale continuity and only then conditioned on independently derived potential-field edges and cover/relief strata.

Why uncatalogued: A concealed permeable structure may alter local radiometric composition without a mapped surface trace. Catalogue distance is not a feature or target input.

Named non-fault mimic: Non-fault lithologic or weathering contact; GeoDAWN flight-line leveling or variable terrain clearance.

Different from existing code: HCE includes simple K/Th and U/Th log ratios and phase congruence; THCS/FCHD include radiometric ratios and gradients. The proposed incremental test is a local multichannel closure/manifold residual with explicit stratification, not another raw ratio or generic edge. Recheck active/shared code before implementation.

Cost/access: low-medium if pinned GeoDAWN layers are usable; owner-mirrored files exist in the manifest; source bytes/AOI suitability not independently re-downloaded or authenticated

Rank 2 · H2-XCATCH · NOT TESTED

Cross-catchment channel-profile discontinuity

Layers: official 3DEP DEM, flow network, lithology/area controls, independent potential-field orientation

Mechanism: Fault-related uplift, offset, or basin-margin hinge may create repeatable stream-power/knickpoint discontinuities across neighboring catchments, including where an individual surface scarp is weak.

Signature: Matched, lithology- and contributing-area-conditioned channel-profile residuals across adjacent catchments, with coherent structural orientation and independent potential-field corroboration.

Why uncatalogued: A shared cross-catchment discontinuity may reveal a concealed or incompletely mapped structure, without using catalogue proximity as evidence.

Named non-fault mimic: Resistant bedrock/lithologic contact or nontectonic base-level change; roads, dams, and drainage edits are additional artifacts.

Different from existing code: FCHD includes chi-channel and step metrics and other lanes include scarp features. The incremental question is paired cross-catchment profile residuals beyond single-channel metrics; a DEM-derived line is not itself a fault label.

Cost/access: medium-high; official USGS 3DEP products are public, but exact AOI coverage, DEM version, and reproducible raw download are unverified

Rank 3 · H3-ANISO-THERM · NOT TESTED

Sampling-corrected, strike-anisotropic shallow-thermal evidence

Layers: DOE GDR 1391 shallow-temperature probes, well/spring temperatures, independent geophysical or strain orientation, observation-density surface

Mechanism: If shallow heat anomalies are transported along fault-controlled permeability, their spatial support may be elongated along an independently estimated structural direction rather than isotropic.

Signature: Observation-density-normalized thermal residuals with a preregistered anisotropic kernel aligned only to independent geophysical/strain orientation, compared against a matched-bandwidth isotropic baseline.

Why uncatalogued: Thermal evidence can reveal a concealed fluid pathway without using the existing fault geometry; fold-local visible-only geometry remains mandatory for any catalogue-derived channels.

Named non-fault mimic: Sparse/biased sampling around known hot springs and wells; basin-scale heat flow or lithologic conductivity contrasts.

Different from existing code: THCS/FCHD already rasterize GDR observations with isotropic/Gaussian smoothing. The incremental question is anisotropy plus explicit sampling-density correction, not merely adding GDR features.

Cost/access: medium if the pinned GDR archive is suitable; official GDR page verified; exact local archive hashes, AOI coverage, and sampling-bias diagnostics remain unverified

Rank 4 · H4-XOVER · NOT TESTED

Acquisition-crossing repeatability of airborne anomalies

Layers: GeoDAWN magnetic/radiometric flight-line observations, tie-line/cross-over geometry, independent potential-field channel

Mechanism: A real subsurface edge should persist across independent survey directions after line-specific correction, whereas line-aligned acquisition noise should not.

Signature: Estimate per-track offsets and cross-over residuals from raw line metadata; require the same narrow anomaly in independent crossing tracks and corroboration in another physical channel.

Why uncatalogued: Repeatability across acquisition geometries can identify real subsurface structure without using mapped-fault proximity.

Named non-fault mimic: A real lithologic contact that is not a fault; tie-line leveling, flight-line striping, and terrain-clearance variation.

Different from existing code: THCS includes raster-domain east-west destriping. This is a distinct proposed test on observation geometry and independent crossings; raw track metadata must be confirmed first.

Cost/access: high; USGS confirms line and tie-line products exist; AOI-specific raw metadata and availability in current owner mirrors are unverified

Rank 5 · H5-LANDSAT · NOT TESTED

Landsat surface-alteration texture conditioned on independent structure

Layers: USGS Landsat Collection 2 surface reflectance, season/cloud/vegetation masks, independent geophysical and relief context

Mechanism: Persistent clay/iron-oxide or related surface contrasts may mark hydrothermal alteration above permeable structures where geophysics alone is ambiguous.

Signature: Pre-register season-matched, cloud/snow-screened alteration contrasts; test spatial texture/orientation only against independent structure and same-lithology/cover-matched controls.

Why uncatalogued: An altered surface footprint can provide independent evidence above a concealed permeable fault without treating spectra alone as a fault label.

Named non-fault mimic: Host-rock lithology/soil differences, seasonal moisture, vegetation, mine disturbance, and exposed playa.

Different from existing code: No exact Landsat alteration-texture feature was identified in the reviewed feature libraries; search all active/shared code before any implementation and treat this as a new data-family hypothesis only if suitable inputs exist.

Cost/access: high; USGS product catalog verified; suitable cloud-free AOI scenes, acquisition dates, licensing terms, and co-registration are unverified
