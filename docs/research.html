Five prospective hypotheses

Hypotheses 01–04 below are from an earlier session and were not built. Hypothesis 05 (SMSR) was proposed, pre-registered, then actually implemented and validated this session.

05 · Geodetic shear-deficit × seismicity (SMSR, implemented)

Combines geodetic strain-rate shear-deficit anisotropy with declustered seismicity kernel density — a pairing none of the nine focus-site files or prior lanes used. HOLDOUT-DTI 0.3213. Pre-registration: knowledge/preregistration-2026-10-28-smsr.md.

01 · Signed potential-field profiles

Magnetic and gravity edge profiles: remove background, test signed asymmetry repeated along strike. Concealed dipping boundaries are a possible target; lithologic contacts or remanence can mimic them. Medium cost; input semantics and official provenance remain unverified.

02 · Magnetic / scarp offset

Compare signed displacement between magnetic edges and elevation slope breaks across repeated profiles. Erosion and alluvial boundaries are confounders. Medium cost; cached alignment must be verified.

03 · Basin-interface residual

Fit a plausible depth/gravity basin response and inspect coherent residual curvature. Interpolation seams and lateral density changes can mimic faults. High cost; density and depth provenance are unresolved.

04 · Flight-line repeatability

Seek edge signatures recurring across height-normalized flight and tie-line crossings. Acquisition artifacts, dikes and non-fault contacts are alternatives. High cost; raw official observations were not obtained in this session.
