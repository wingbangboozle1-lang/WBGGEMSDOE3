Ranked new hypotheses

Layers, signature, off-catalogue rationale, mimics and cost

Full text, official sources and the frozen plan: preregistration-2026-10-27.md ↗

01 · EXECUTED — SGCE

Stress-gated along-strike continuation + metric-optimal coverage

Layers: cached geodetic strain/dilatation, detrended elevation and slope, magnetic tilt/gradient, gravity gradient, conductivity, basement depth, seismicity, LiDAR scarp bands, K/Th/U, SGMC distance; fold-local PCA strike/endpoint/relay geometry of visible faults. Signature: rank-bin naive-Bayes susceptibility × exp(−dvisible/15 px) × strain gate; Dinkelbach coverage placement under the exact DTI kernel. Off-catalogue rationale: unmapped faults are commonly continuations/relays of mapped systems with subdued scarps. Mimic: lithologic contacts, survey stripes, fan/road edges. Cost: medium, no new data.

02 · NOT RUN — low cost

Radiometric–magnetic cross-gradient null

A magnetic tilt/gradient edge with no co-located K/Th/U edge and low relief suggests a buried contact. All layers cached. Mimic: nonmagnetic lithologic contact, variable aircraft terrain clearance (USGS warning).

03 · NOT RUN — low cost

Anisotropic seismicity-alignment continuation

Smooth earthquake distance/intensity along the local maximum strain axis, then ridge extraction. Mimic: blast/mining clusters, cultural noise.

04 · DATA-LIMITED — high cost

Thermal/conductance pathway contrast

2 m probes and well/spring temperatures normalized by sampling density, intersected with conductivity gradients. Needs GDR 1391 (public CC BY 4.0; a partial hash-pinned mirror exists here). Mimic: groundwater, conductivity contrasts, biased sampling. AOI fitness unverified.

05 · NOT VALIDATABLE HERE

Final-round diversity coverage

Phase 2 labels expand after expert review of all submissions, so novel plausible structures may gain. Consensus over other teams' rasters would leak visible catalogue faults in hide-and-recover, so it cannot be scored honestly on this holdout. Listed as future work only.
