Holdout protocol

  • Four hard spatial folds (NW/NE/SW/SE). A fold's entire catalogue quadrant is withheld.
  • Truth = withheld pixels more than 500 m from any visible fault, so recovery cannot be earned by predicting next to a visible trace.
  • Scoring uses the official formula (α = 0.2, β = 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel) with the official masking of visible faults, implemented in the pinned shared template (gems57.metric) and verified against a literal O(|G|·|P|) transcription of the published equations.
  • Controls: oracle (dots = truth) returns 1.0000 on every fold; random dots return 0.0898.
  • Leakage canary: every plane alone, scored against the fold truth, has AUC 0.44-0.63 — below the 0.90 leakage bar, and in the range expected for 100 m single-feature lineament detectors.

That is a real limitation of this release and it is stated as such — but under the official formula coverage is not cosmetic, it multiplies recall.
