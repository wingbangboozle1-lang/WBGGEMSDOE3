Leakage canaries

No feature was exempted from a canary check. The strongest single-field association with the public catalogue in this stack is far below the 0.90 leakage threshold, so the fields are describing geology, not memorising the labels. The one legitimate label-derived input — distance to the catalogue — is used only to build the mask (the organizer masks those cells), never as a positive predictor, and it is recomputed from visible faults inside the holdout.

Recorded in evidence/ledger_inversion.json and knowledge/preregistration-2026-10-26.md.

What would actually move the needle

  • New information, not new transforms. The ledger's 118 files show a hard ceiling near 0.28 for anything derived from the existing 19 bands. The public leader at 0.4544 must be using something absent from these mirrors — most plausibly much finer structural evidence (1 m lidar lineament vectors rather than the rasterised scarp stack), InSAR strain, or a newer fault compilation.
  • Free, official and obtainable: USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM tiles (tile-level download works without a login), USGS Quaternary Fault and Fold Database, and the GDR 1391 INGENIOUS compilation already used here. Any of them must be fetched and licence-checked before a hypothesis that depends on them is called viable.
  • The honest ceiling of this project: with the pinned mirrors it can estimate scores to ±0.037 and rank them at 0.946 Spearman, but it cannot manufacture the missing 0.15 DTI.
