3. Irregularities to resolve before another experiment

  1. The literal duplicate rule is made vacuous by a registered dense positive raster. A content-aware reading is a protocol change, not an automatic waiver.
  2. The skarpline candidate records PROXY-DTI rather than the requested whole-segment hide-and-recover HOLDOUT-DTI.
  3. Historical values are owner-reported claims without organizer receipts bound to exact file bytes.
  4. The data mirror proves pinned byte identity but not organizer provenance; the official data tab is login-walled in this environment.
  5. The official outside-footprint convention and the historic portal range rejection have not been confirmed by a portal receipt.
