Limitations we are not hiding

  • HOLDOUT-DTI is a local proxy, not a leaderboard score: all nine focus rasters score at or below 0.105 on these folds while their owners report 0.24-0.28 on the platform.
  • The organizer's withheld subset is a different population from the holdout's withheld 20 % of the published catalogue, so the transfer is plausible but unproven.
  • Registry completeness is not claimable (28 of 522 entries audited); see the scope statement in uniqueness.
  • The candidate reproduces a corridor geometry, not a physical rock-property model; a corridor prior would also fire on non-fault linear features.
  • The competition deadline is internally inconsistent (homepage 23:59 UTC vs rules 5 p.m. ET); the earlier time should be assumed.
