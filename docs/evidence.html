Known gaps — read before trusting anything

  • No organizer receipt exists for any file in this repository. Every sibling score is OWNER-REPORTED.
  • The hidden truth field is not observable; the posterior is a model of it, validated only against sibling scores (site-grouped CV), not against truth.
  • The catalogue hide-and-recover holdout previously used here is anti-correlated with live scores (ρ = −0.85 over the nine focus files) and is retained for provenance only.
  • Data provenance: labels.tif / existing_faults.tif (SHA-256 7ba308cc…) and sample_submission.tif (2176d08e…) are byte-identical to the copies committed in buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE24 at data/bridge/; the External rasters are documented in that repository's data/manifest.json. Byte identity proves integrity against a mirror, not organizer origin.
