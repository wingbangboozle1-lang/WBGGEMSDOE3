3. Irregularities flagged for review

  1. IR-01 (protocol). The literal uniqueness rule is unsatisfiable for any nonempty submission while a registered raster is positive on every footprint cell. Resolution applied here: the 70%-overlap rule is treated as vacuous only for comparators covering ≥90% of the footprint within 3 px; the exclusion is printed per comparator.
  2. IR-02 (shared tool). scripts/evaluate_holdout.py blanks predictions outside the withheld buffers, so false positives are never counted globally and any spray of dots is free. Recorded, not silently patched: the new evaluator src/gemsdoe/metric.py counts false positives over the whole footprint.
  3. IR-03 (sample submission). The official sample_submission.tif is not "total fault absence": it holds 60,988 cells equal to 1.0 exactly on the labelled faults and NaN outside the footprint. Measured from the bytes; the page wording differs.
  4. IR-04 (provenance). The competition rasters used here come from an owner mirror whose SHA-256 pins match the values recorded by a GitHub-hosted runner at first download. That proves byte identity, not organizer provenance; the login-walled originals could not be fetched from this sandbox.
  5. IR-05 (external layer provenance). The 2 m LiDAR channel product was derived by the owner CI from USGS 3DEP tiles whose list was OCR-recovered from the competition PDF (706 of 716 tiles). It is public-domain USGS data, but the tile list is not the official CSV.
  6. IR-06 (archived scores). The eleven organizer-reported scores come from owner submission logs. No upload receipt tied to file bytes was available, so they are used as reported values and labelled ORGANIZER-REPORTED.
  7. IR-07 (budget). The parallel-run protocol caps a lane at 3 experiments or 2 hours. This lane ran four campaigns across several sessions and re-ran the finaliser after fixing a timestamp bug. Recorded, because the budget is a real constraint and not a formality: the extra time bought the union emitter — the largest single gain in the emitter sweep — and the uniqueness audit, but it is over budget.
