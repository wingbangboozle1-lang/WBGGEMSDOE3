Why GEMSDOE32?

What the bytes show — and what they do not

Observed construction

The pinned H33-B2 support is a subset of the GEMSDOE28 H27-4 base: 2,545 base pixels were removed, all approximately 1.41–2 pixels from the mirrored mapped-fault raster. This is a construction measurement, not evidence of score causality.

Reasonable mechanism

The stated distance-tolerant Tversky metric balances coverage of hidden faults against false-positive mass. Sparse placement or pruning could improve that trade-off, but might also remove useful predictions. Hidden labels and an uploaded-file receipt are unavailable; neither the precise reason for the reported 0.2778 nor a higher future score can be established here.

Higher is possible, not demonstrated

Public leaderboard observations show other teams have achieved larger values; they do not establish what any of these files will score. A comparable, leak-checked holdout and then an organizer receipt are necessary. The task is fault mapping; predicted faults are not proof of geothermal vents.
