Why GEMSDOE32 B2 scores 0.2778 — and what beats it

B2 is not a new model: it is a byte-exact pruning chain of the dotted h19-5 family (verified this session with np.array_equal on the pinned bytes):

  • d2-8 (44,090 dots) → 0.2600
  • minus 3,891 dots on the mapped catalogue → solo-d28 (40,199) → 0.2708 (+0.0108)
  • minus 2,545 dots in the 1.01–2.01 px catalogue band → B2 (37,654) → 0.2778 (+0.0070)
  • plus 1,200 extra dots (anderson-pinn, 38,854) → 0.2750 (−0.0028)

The organizer masks catalogue truth pixels, but predictions there still cost FP mass — so catalogue dots are pure loss. Dots whose 300 m kernel credit is duplicated by neighbours add FP mass without TP credit. The metric is won by precision of placement, not coverage. The entire h19-5 network is already saturated by the family (0 network cells lie >3 px from family dots), so a unique submission must spend ≥30% of its dots off-network — the uniqueness tax. That is why honest estimates for unique candidates cluster at 0.23–0.27 and why beating 0.2778 requires new truth-location information (the H2/H3 research lanes), not more family pruning.
