Why GEMSDOE32 scored 0.2778, and whether we can beat it

What the bytes show — and what a new, independent attempt achieved

Observed construction

The pinned H33-B2 support is a subset of the GEMSDOE28/31 H27-4 base: 2,545 base pixels were removed, all approximately 1.41–2 pixels from the mirrored mapped-fault raster. Under the α=0.2/β=0.8 distance-weighted Tversky metric, mapped faults are masked from scoring, so near-catalogue false positives are almost pure penalty; pruning them plausibly explains the reported rise to 0.2778. This is a construction measurement, not a proof of causality (hidden labels were not available to us).

SMSR's independent attempt

Every top focus-site score is a pruning/re-weighting variant of the same dotted-ridge family (confirmed by their mutual >97% dot overlap). SMSR instead adds a geophysical signal none of those nine files use — geodetic strain-rate anisotropy plus declustered seismicity — fit with a spatially-blocked, leakage-canary-checked model on the real official feature stack, reaching HOLDOUT-DTI 0.3213 on our own instrument (Δ+0.0040 vs. the shipped-feature baseline in this mode).

What is, and is not, proven

User-supplied public leaderboard observations of 0.3195 and later 0.4413 are personal, time-dependent observations, not fetched receipts, and are not reproduced here. HOLDOUT-DTI is our own proxy measurement with a documented inherited leakage-canary caveat; only an actual DrivenData receipt establishes an ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED score.
