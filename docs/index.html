What the bytes show — and what they do not

The reported 0.2778 is an OWNER-REPORTED historical value; no receipt linking it to these exact bytes is available here.

Observed construction

Decoded-byte comparison: GEMSDOE32 H33-B2 has 37,654 positive cells and is a subset of the GEMSDOE28 H27-4 base with 40,199. The H33-B2 support removes 2,545 cells; their measured distance from the mirrored catalogue ranges from 1.414 to 2.0 pixels. This is a raster construction measurement, not a score or score-causality test.

Plausible, unproven explanation

If the documented mask/kernel behavior matches organizer scoring, pruning predictions close to already mapped faults could reduce low-value false-positive mass while retaining some distance-tolerant credit. It could also remove credit for hidden faults. The reported score change is consistent with that trade-off, but the hidden labels and organizer receipt are unavailable, so the cause of OWNER-REPORTED 0.2778 is not established.

Not a basis for a new score claim

The nine focus TIFFs are not unique under the recorded pairwise 3-pixel comparison; the universal mirrored witness also triggers the controlling literal stop for any nonempty in-footprint file. A different geological mechanism might perform differently, but no new candidate or comparable holdout has been tested. Fault predictions are not proof of geothermal vents.
