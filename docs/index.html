1 · DEM curvature-break persistence

DEM profile/plan curvature and slope at multiple scales. Persistent aligned breaks may extend beyond mapped traces. Mimics: lithologic contacts, erosional scarps. Low–medium effort.

2 · Magnetic edge coherence

GeoDawn magnetic channels: analytic-signal gradient and directional coherence. Concealed basement discontinuity candidate. Mimics: intrusive/lithologic boundaries, survey seams. Medium effort.

Verified K/eTh/eU-equivalent channels with robust normalization and cross-channel consensus. Mimics: regolith, moisture, vegetation, striping. Medium effort; channel metadata needed.

Coherent DEM and magnetic edges in independent modalities. Mimics: contacts and chance alignment. Medium effort.

What makes a submission eligible?

  1. Authorized competition inputs and official sample/template raster are present.
  2. Whole-segment buffered hide-and-recover evaluation, exact masking, pooled DTI (alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel), withheld-positive count, evaluator version and 95% CI are recorded.
  3. Each feature passes the leakage canary (single-feature AUC >0.90 is investigated as leakage).
  4. Surface and final-dot novelty checks: correlation ≤0.90 and no more than 70% of dots within 3 px of any registry item.
  5. Generated one-band TIFF has values in [0,1] inside the footprint and matching CRS, shape and transform; NaN policy and SHA-256 are recorded.
  6. Independent validator passes. Only then can a clearly labeled download be exposed. Competition submission itself occurs on DrivenData.
