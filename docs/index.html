What this file is

A corridor band around the published catalogue, not a copy of anything

The candidate places 75,671 dots in the 3–12 px band away from the organizer's published fault traces, on the ridge of a σ = 24 px Gaussian density of those traces, thinned to a 4 px minimum separation. It never emits a dot on a known fault and never leaves the sample footprint.

Why not a copy?

The map is a deterministic function of data/official/labels.tif (the published catalogue) and four fixed hyperparameters. No registry raster, no hidden label and no other team's submission enters the pipeline. That is also why its 3-px overlap is high-but-legal rather than accidental.

Why a corridor at all?

Two facts drive it: the DTI charges a dot only within 3 px of a truth pixel, and the organizer's withheld faults are new strands of existing systems. The holdout confirms that withheld fault pixels cluster 3–12 px off the visible traces — the distance profile in Evidence — so a band, not a trace-follower, is where the recall is.

What it is not

Not a rock-property model. The physics layers were screened and could not beat geometry on the holdout; the β-modulated geophysics variant helped only one fold. That negative result is kept in the record rather than papered over.

Format gate

A previous file in this repository was rejected by the portal with “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”. This candidate was re-read from the saved bytes: min 0.0, max 1.0, no NaN, no Inf, single band, float32, matching CRS/shape/transform, and every positive cell inside the sample footprint.
