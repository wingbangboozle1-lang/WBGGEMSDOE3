Steps at the competition portal

01

Confirm an approved, unique file

Open this page and verify that the automatic candidate, feature canaries, same-evaluator holdout, full literal registry checks, saved-file validator and separate weekly selector all pass. The parallel candidate fails the registry and footprint checks; no release has cleared the full sequence.

02

Download the exact approved GeoTIFF

Use the download shown beside its SHA-256 and compare the bytes with its receipt. The file must be one float32 band, match the sample CRS, shape and transform, and have no nonfinite or out-of-range values in the footprint. The official text requires null/NaN outside bounds, whereas the shared writer emits unmasked zeros outside the mirrored footprint. Confirm the meaning of bounds with organizers and reconcile the shared writer before upload.

03

Check available weekly slots

Sign in to DrivenData GEMS. The official rules state up to three submissions per week; check the live portal and team quota before using one. We cannot see your account quota.

04

Upload once, with the approved note

Select “Submit” then “Make new submission”, choose the exact TIFF or single-TIFF ZIP, and paste the generated name/note if provided (note ≤ 140 characters in this project). Preserve the organizer receipt and uploaded hash together. Do not report a local holdout as a portal result.

05

Select one final submission before the deadline

The same selected submission is scored for both prize rounds. Review eligibility, reproducible code, external-data licensing and the required disclosure of generative AI use in the official rules.

About the range error

“Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” can reflect invalid values or an encoding issue, but the exact cause of the reported rejection has not been established. The writer rejects NaN, infinities, sentinels and values outside [0,1] rather than silently clipping predictions. The official text says outside bounds must be null/NaN, but the mirrored sample has NaNs outside its footprint and the shared writer emits unmasked zeros. The meaning of bounds and acceptance are unresolved; investigate with organizers and reconcile the shared writer before any future upload.

Automated checks are necessary, not sufficient

A file passing the local validator is only structurally compatible with the mirrored sample. It still needs uniqueness, leakage, scientifically valid holdout, and selector approval. The hidden test labels remain inaccessible.
