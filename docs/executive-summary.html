2. Why GEMSDOE32 B2 (0.2778) led the nine sites

GEMSDOE32's B2 file has 37,654 positive cells and is an exact subset of the GEMSDOE31 base (40,199 cells): 3,891 on-catalogue dots were removed (+0.0108 holdout delta), then 2,545 more dots in the 1–2 px catalogue band were removed (+0.0070). Under the pooled distance-weighted Tversky metric (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel), removing redundant near-catalogue prediction mass can raise DTI because the organizer excludes known-fault pixels from scoring — dots there earn no true-positive credit while still adding false-positive mass.

This is a byte-level observation, not a proven causal explanation. The hidden labels and upload receipt are unavailable, so 0.2778 cannot be independently verified. No local estimate or projection can substitute for an organizer receipt.

An off-catalogue blind-fault probability map. Why it is unique

The focus-site dotted family clusters dots adjacent to known catalogue faults. Our dots sit off-catalogue near thermal features with lidar scarp support — a different region of predictor space. Full step-by-step guide: how-to-submit.html

  1. Resolve the repository-wide DO-NOT-SUBMIT gate (literal registry stop) and obtain separate selector approval for the exact SHA-256.
  2. Download the exact TIFF above (verify SHA-256 matches).
  3. Go to DrivenData → New submission.
  4. Upload the single-band GeoTIFF (.tif) or ZIP containing one GeoTIFF.
  5. Add a unique note (≤140 characters), e.g. "Off-catalogue lidar-scarp x thermal blind faults, k=25 clustering".
