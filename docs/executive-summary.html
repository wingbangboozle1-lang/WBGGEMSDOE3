Why did 0.2778 win? (verified line by line from the bytes)

  1. It is an exact subset of the 0.2708 base. GEMSDOE32 …e5eb6e7e-zeros contains 37,654 dots; GEMSDOE28 h27-4 contains 40,199. Set difference: 0 dots added, 2,545 removed — verified with champion_is_subset_of_base = True.
  2. All 2,545 removed dots lie within 3 px of the public catalogue (fraction within 3 px = 1.0; 1,201 at √2 px and 1,344 at 2.0 px; none on catalogue pixels). The 3,891 dots removed earlier, from GEMSDOE25 to GEMSDOE28, sit at 1.0 px (adjacent), not on the catalogue. The premise that these dots earned zero credit (the scored truth is off-catalogue) is an assumption, not verified.
  3. That clean edit is a two-equation experiment. With T unchanged, the pair gives M = 0.2(s₁N₁ − s₂N₂)/(0.8(s₂ − s₁)) = 15,200.3 hidden truth cells and T = s₂(0.2N₂ + 0.8M) = 5,470.2 cells reached. Δ predicted = +0.0071, observed = +0.0070.
  4. The same model reproduces six more independent files (different sites, sessions, model families) with implied T between 5,230 and 5,481 — see the table above and evidence/pair_identification.json.
  5. So the leaderboard is dot-budget arithmetic. Adding a dot raises the denominator by exactly 0.2 and the numerator by its kernel credit k; a dot pays only if k > 0.2·DTI ≈ 0.056. Everything above 0.2778 has to come from the credit rate, i.e. from placing dots where the unmapped faults actually are.

Can we beat 0.2778? Yes — the mechanism and the risk

Our file raises the credit rate two ways: (i) it deletes the entire zero-yield collar around the catalogue (measured 0.000% of our dots are within 3 px of it) and (ii) it ranks ground by a tomographic posterior fitted to 118 probes (tracked fit, evidence/ledger_inversion.json; the earlier 98-probe count is not reproducible from tracked files) plus independent LiDAR-scarp / magnetic / radiometric saliency, then spends a bounded budget of 20,000 dots at 3 px minimum separation.

Risk, stated plainly: the posterior's absolute level is not identifiable; the site-grouped cross-validated score model has ρ = 0.946 and MAE = 0.037 (n=118, tracked fit), and the calibration of the design predictor has R² = 0.39. If our dots are no better than the sibling rate we land near 0.18. No slot is spent while the decision is HOLD. Resolve the uniqueness reading first; then any selected file still needs its own gate and the organizer receipt decides.
