GEMSDOE32 0.2778: the defensible interpretation

OWNER-REPORTED 0.2778 is not linked to an authenticated submission receipt in this repository.

The pinned raster audit records GEMSDOE32 H33-B2 as a subset of the GEMSDOE28 H27-4 base. H33-B2 has 37,654 positive cells versus 40,199 in the base; 2,545 cells were removed, all at measured catalogue distances from 1.414 to 2.000 pixels. This is observed support geometry, not hidden-label truth.

A plausible mechanism is that pruning cells near already mapped faults reduces low-value false-positive mass while retaining distance-kernel credit. That would be compatible with the recorded metric convention, but the removed cells could also be near hidden faults. Without the hidden labels in an authenticated form and a receipt that binds the reported value to these bytes, the causal explanation is not established.

The stored pair analysis algebraically infers hidden-truth quantities from the two OWNER-REPORTED inputs under the repository's assumed binary DTI identity. Those are model-dependent inferred quantities—not observed hidden-truth counts, not a validated score estimate, and not evidence that a new geological method would score higher. Domain boundary

The competition target is fault prediction. Faults may be relevant to fluid pathways, but a fault map alone does not locate a geothermal vent, demonstrate discharge, or establish a viable geothermal resource. The four ideas on the Research page are untested hypotheses with explicit non-fault mimics.
