Research, instruments, sources

Hypothesis and mechanism as run

Hypothesis
Expert-added faults concentrate where the public catalogue is structurally blind: thick alluvial cover with weak surface expression. Emitting on cover-gated basement/gravity/geodetic concordance rather than on ridge strength targets that blind zone.
Mechanism
crest-normalised ridge of |grad(isostatic gravity anomaly)| colinear within 2 px with a depth-to-basement step, weighted by the second invariant + shear rate of the geodetic strain-rate tensor, multiplied by a weakness gate (low detrended-elevation curvature, high basement depth) and a >=3 px separation from mapped traces; DTI-thinned to local crests, level 1.0, zeros outside the footprint.
Named non-fault mimic
lithologic and intrusive contacts produce the same gravity step without faulting; geodetic fields carry atmospheric/thermal InSAR ramps and groundwater or mining subsidence; cover thickness correlates with basins that are simply unmapped at any resolution, which caps recall rather than creating false positives on ridges.
Verdict
negative — uniqueness or validator gate failed (see uniqueness/validator blocks)
Limits
no organizer receipt; hidden labels unavailable; proxy is an external catalogue, not the private test set; registry census covers accessible public mirrors only; the weekly slot was NOT used and no upload was attempted; fast_pair vs shared compare disagreement recorded in uniqueness.guard_caveat; the weekly slot was not used and no upload was attempted
