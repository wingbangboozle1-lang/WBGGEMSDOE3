Hypothesis

Collinear edges in independent hydrothermal/radiometric, magnetic, gravity, strain and topographic channels mark fluid-carrying faults whose subtle traces are missing from a catalogue built from mapped scarps; late experiment E4 selects a saturated dotted lattice over the top-q physical-susceptibility region.

Mechanism

56 static physical fields (incl. structure-tensor cross-channel phase congruence) -> spatially blocked HistGradientBoostingClassifier on visible faults only -> probability field -> fixed 3 px lattice restricted to the top-30 % susceptibility -> binary p=1 dots.

Mimic

Potassium-rich lithologic contacts or variable-clearance GeoDAWN radiometric survey stripes (flight azimuth 90 deg) that are collinear with magnetic and gravity edges, plus roads and erosional lineaments.
