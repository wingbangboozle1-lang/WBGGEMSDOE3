What failed and why it mattered. E2/E3 scored 0.0236/0.0231 because 100% of their dots sat within 5 px of a visible fault: the fold-legal catalogue-geometry features dominated the boosted model. E4 dropped those two features and switched from Dinkelbach allocation to a saturated dotted lattice over the top-q physical susceptibility region; q and spacing were chosen only on the independent off-catalogue SGMC population, then scored once on the hide-and-recover holdout. That is the registered experiment, not a post-hoc search.
