Why 0.2778 happened, and can we beat it?

The B2 file is an exact 37,654-pixel subset of a 40,199-pixel H27-4 base: 2,545 dots close to mapped faults were removed. With α=0.2 / β=0.8 and a 300 m triangular kernel, a sparse dotted network gets near-full credit for nearby truth while paying little for false positives, and pruning catalogue traces removes predictions that cannot be in the hidden new-fault set. That is a mechanism in the files, not a receipt-verified causal explanation. Our E4 experiment beats the same-evaluator champion proxy on a fold-legal holdout (0.1292 vs 0.0992, paired 95% CI +0.0177 to +0.0417); but the released, uniqueness-repaired file scores 0.0931 on a static read-back holdout, and the owner-reported ledger projection (0.082) does not support beating 0.2778.
