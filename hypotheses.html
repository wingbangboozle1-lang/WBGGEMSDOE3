Best feasible first test		 Depth-consistent basement step plus kinematic support
Potential-field and strain-rate bands: depth_to_base_surf, tmi_vg, iso_grav_anom_vg, geod_shearrate, geod_dilaterate, optionally geod_2ndinv and rtp.		 Require a coherent step/boundary across magnetic and gravity features plus aligned shear/dilation. Buried basement offset and localized strain can persist where surface traces are unmapped. Train and score only against fold-visible catalogue data; keep the target's withheld segment and buffer isolated. Not just a thresholded lineament, a single-sensor ridge, or a new thinning rule: require cross-domain depth/kinematic agreement and reject isolated edges. Related geophysical, Euler/depth, and strain approaches exist; the exact composite was not found in the inspected focus-site summaries, but global novelty is not proven. Band semantics must be checked against the canonical metadata before use. Lithologic contact, basin margin, intrusive boundary, or airborne flight-line/leveling seam. Medium, conditional. Most feasible because the bands appear in the owner-maintained prepared manifest, but no numeric gain is justified. Medium. No new external dataset if the shared feature cache contains these channels. The cache is absent here; the official inputs and band metadata have not been restored. BLOCKED

New external data		 Multi-epoch InSAR deformation-gradient persistence
Sentinel-1 line-of-sight displacement and coherence time series, e.g. NASA/JPL OPERA DISP-S1.		 Search for persistent, localized shear/extension gradients across repeat acquisitions. Deformation associated with active concealed structures can reveal faults absent from a surface-fault compilation. Distinct from static potential-field lineaments and point temperature records. No time-series InSAR displacement layer was found in the reviewed focus-site summaries. This is not a claim that no related work exists anywhere. Groundwater withdrawal, landslides, geothermal reservoir compaction, atmospheric delay, decorrelation, or infrastructure motion. Medium–high if faults are active; low if dormant. Not a leaderboard projection. High. Named source/product: NASA/JPL OPERA DISP-S1 via NASA Earthdata. The product landing page, host access, coverage, and terms were not checked here; not viable until access is verified. BLOCKED

New external data		 Geochemical mixing and geothermometry at springs/wells
DOE GDR, USGS NWIS, or NGDS chemistry if location/metadata coverage is adequate; candidate measures include silica, chloride/boron, alkali ratios, and stable isotopes.		 Look for coherent fluid-composition gradients or mixing fronts along off-catalogue corridors. Deep geothermal fluids may rise through permeable fault pathways not mapped at the surface. The reviewed records discuss probe-temperature points and mapped geothermal manifestations; they do not establish a well-curated chemical-composition layer. This tests fluid chemistry rather than reusing the same point locations. Bedrock chemistry, evaporation, shallow-water mixing, sparse sampling, well bias, or human contamination. Medium, potentially specific but sparse. No numeric estimate. High. Official starting points: DOE GDR submission 1391 and USGS NWIS. The pages and chemistry coverage were not checked. BLOCKED

New external data		 Seasonally normalized land-surface thermal residual
Multiyear Landsat Collection 2 surface temperature plus vegetation/land-cover covariates.		 Estimate persistent warm/cool anomalies after controlling for season, elevation, aspect, and land cover. Fault-fed geothermal discharge can create repeated thermal/vegetation anomalies near concealed conduits. Different from static airborne radiometrics and point temperature records. The reviewed materials do not verify a multitemporal satellite-temperature anomaly test; exact novelty remains uncertain. Solar exposure, irrigation, roads/urban heat, wildfire, emissivity, and seasonal soil moisture. Low–medium. Surface expressions are intermittent and confounded; not a score forecast. High. Official source: USGS Landsat Collection 2. The host and footprint coverage were not checked. BLOCKED
